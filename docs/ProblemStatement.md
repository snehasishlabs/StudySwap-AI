# Problem Statement

## Context
Every academic year, millions of students enrolled in universities, colleges, and competitive exam coaching hubs (such as UPSC, NEET, JEE, GATE, SSC) face immense financial strain purchasing textbooks, reference guides, and stationary. 

## Key Pain Points

1. **Escalating Costs of Study Materials**:
   - Standard competitive exam guides and academic reference books cost between ₹500 and ₹3,000 per book. Students easily spend over ₹15,000 to ₹25,000 annually on physical books.
   - Once an exam is cleared or a semester ends, these books become obsolete for the owner but carry high residual value for incoming students.

2. **Information Asymmetry & Fragmented Discovery**:
   - Finding reliable handwritten topper notes or specific book editions relies on word-of-mouth, scattered WhatsApp study groups, or untrusted classifieds.
   - Buyers have no way to verify the condition, completeness, or authenticity of notes before meeting.

3. **Massive Academic Paper Waste**:
   - Tons of pristine study materials, solved question banks, and reference books are sent to scrap dealers or discarded after each exam cycle, creating unnecessary environmental waste.

4. **Inefficient Negotiation & Handoff**:
   - Coordinating prices, verifying student identity, and arranging safe campus meetups is cumbersome and lacks structure.
